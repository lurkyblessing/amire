why i started amiré

bless founded amiré to bridge her love for beauty with her obsession for fashion. as a girlie perpetually in sunglasses, she saw a glaring gap between the accessories we wear and the routines we live by, and decided to fill it herself.

after six years of exploring the coolest niches in marketing, it was time to create an even cooler one. the vision behind amiré is simple: standing out, taking up space, and becoming the most creative version of yourself.

we shouldn't have to compromise our aesthetic just to carry our essentials, nor should we have to dig through a bag for a touch-up. every rollout and every new shade release is curated to match the effortless cool girl energy we all exude.

with love & effortless cool,
bless

our philosophy

we turn static everyday accessories into active delivery systems. here is what guides everything we engineer.

the face, engineered.

beauty isn't just what you apply; it is how you move through the world. we merge cosmetic formulation with ergonomic industrial design, putting the product exactly where your hands naturally reach.

unbothered & prepared.

no digging through clutches, no bulky cosmetic bags, no compromised outfits. our designs exist to keep your routine uninterrupted and effortlessly cool all day.

utility patent pending.

we don't slap logos on off-the-shelf catalog hardware. every mechanism, hinge cavity, push system, and protective seal is custom-engineered from the ground up to eliminate waste.
