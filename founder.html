why i started amiré

bless founded amiré to bridge her love for beauty with her obsession for fashion. as a girlie perpetually in sunglasses, she saw a glaring gap between the accessories we wear and the routines we live by, and decided to fill it herself.

after six years of exploring the coolest niches in marketing, it was time to create an even cooler one. the vision behind amiré is simple: standing out, taking up space, and becoming the most creative version of yourself.

we shouldn't have to compromise our aesthetic just to carry our essentials, nor should we have to dig through a bag for a touch-up. every rollout and every new shade release is curated to match the effortless cool girl energy we all exude.

best,
bless

our philosophy

three things guide everything we make.

stand out.

rooted in the yoruba affirmation èmí reé, “here i am.” amiré is about taking up space, standing in your authenticity, and never blending into the background.

stick around.

no digging through clutches, no touch-ups lost at the bottom of a bag. your lip combo lives in your shades, so your look stays with you all day.

be the star.

we don't follow trends, we set them. every rollout and every new shade release is made for the girl who walks in and becomes the main character.

for the girl who refuses to choose.
