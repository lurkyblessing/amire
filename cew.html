why functional beauty?

The concept of functional beauty didn't start in a corporate R&D lab. It started with how we actually move through the world. We shouldn't have to compromise our aesthetic just to carry our essentials, nor should we have to dig through a bag for a touch-up. The things we wear should work for us.

amiré was built on that exact frustration. We are turning static accessories into active delivery systems. Sarafina Shades takes the real estate on your face and makes it functional, putting the formula exactly where your hands naturally go. It is about taking up space, with intention.

*while this is the first product amiré is launching, there are other concepts currently in the stages of design and conceptualization.

the architecture

We didn't slap a logo on off-the-shelf packaging. Sarafina Shades are engineered from the ground up to solve the physical realities of carrying cosmetics inside eyewear, with a strict focus on reducing plastic waste.

Pillar 01

the cartridge system

Eco-Conscious Design: We engineered a swappable, refillable core. Instead of throwing away an entire plastic component when the formula runs out, the user simply replaces the internal cartridge—drastically cutting down on single-use plastics.

Pillar 02

the dispensing mechanism

A custom-engineered pusher system guarantees precision application from the cartridge without compromising the structural integrity of the frame.

Pillar 03

environmental control

Integrated dual sealing rings ensure the formula remains entirely isolated and stable, regardless of outside conditions.

Pillar 04

manufacturing reality

The temple piece wall thickness and internal cavities are mapped to exact CNC machining tolerances, built specifically for scalable injection molding.
